## 1. Client Service Fee Refunds

*(Covers: the 5% client service fee (minimum PKR 300) is refundable only if the project is cancelled before Stage 1 is approved (FR-PAY-07); if Stage 1 is approved, the client fee is non-refundable; partial delivery scenarios — refund of portion attributable to unstarted stages per D17.)*

## 2. Project Cancellation by Client

*(Covers: offence number is derived from cancellations in the last 12 months (D3); cancellation before any stage is submitted — 1st: warning, full refund including fee; 2nd: 5% of agreed price to developer, rest refunded; 3rd+: 10% to developer, strike, account review; cancellation after work has started — same thresholds plus developer receives 25% of the in-progress stage amount; compensation is paid only from escrow held and never charges the client beyond what they already paid (D28); escrow remaining after compensation is refunded to the client.)*

## 3. Project Cancellation by Developer (Before Starting)

*(Covers: within 24 hours of bid acceptance and before any submission — 1st offence: warning; 2nd+: strike and reliability score drop of 10 points (FR-CAN-04); project returns to open; escrow and fee held — client may request a full refund or wait for a new bid (D35, FR-CAN-05); once a new bid is accepted, escrow is reused, fee recalculated and positive differences paid via a new 48-hour window (D35).)*

## 4. Developer Abandonment

*(Covers: abandonment = developer quits after first submission, or client ends the assignment because the developer is 8+ days late after grace (D2, D36); approved stages stay paid to the developer; a submitted but unapproved stage is put to the client for a decision within 3 days — approve and pay, or reject and refund (FR-ABN-01); unsubmitted stages are not paid; remaining escrow is refunded to the client; project reopens; for reopen pricing see section 6.)*

## 5. Late Delivery Penalties

*(Covers: 24-hour grace period after stage due date (BR-09); late penalty rates — 2% per day for days 1-3, 3% per day from day 4, cumulative, capped at 25% of the stage amount (D13, BR-10); penalty is deducted from the developer's stage payout and returned to the client as a discount — the platform does not keep it; lateness measured on first submission; revisions requested by the client do not add lateness; each day after the grace period counts as a full day regardless of the hour (D13).)*

## 6. Project Reopen Pricing

*(Covers: when a project reopens after abandonment, the AI recalculates price and time for remaining stages (FR-ABN-04); the new agreed price may differ from the original; difference = new_agreed_remaining − escrow_still_held; if positive, client pays the difference through the normal 48-hour payment window; if negative, the extra is refunded to the client immediately as a refund payments row; client fee applies only to a positive difference (D17).)*

## 7. Dispute-Resolved Refunds

*(Covers: if a dispute is resolved by admin in favour of the client (full or partial), a refund payments row is created for the client's share; if resolved in favour of the developer, the escrow is released to the developer; a split creates both a release and a refund row in the same transaction (FR-DSP-01).)*

## 8. Escrow and Settlement Timeline

*(Covers: escrow is held until a stage is approved or a dispute is resolved; no interest is paid on escrowed funds; payouts are scheduled for the next business day after a stage is approved (BR-03, D6, D12); payouts are currently processed manually — platform admins transfer funds via bank or mobile wallet within one business day of the payout being scheduled (D25, FR-PAY-09).)*

## 9. How to Request a Refund

*(Covers: refunds for cancellations are processed automatically by the platform; disputes — raise through the Dispute mechanism on the milestone page; for any other refund query, open a support ticket.)*
